Luminox Automation — IoT Engineers by Trade

Luminox Automation is a Mumbai-based IoT engineering company founded in 2019 by Fahad Khan, an electrical engineer specialising in IoT systems. We design, build, and deploy connected hardware products and smart infrastructure systems for clients across India, the GCC, and global markets. Our work spans custom IoT product development, smart building automation, industrial monitoring systems, PCB and hardware design, cloud connectivity, and AgriTech sensor solutions.

Our Vision

To be the engineering partner that proves world-class IoT — from patent-pending smart homes to industrial-grade wearables — can be built in India and deployed anywhere in the world.

Our Mission

To empower people to have total control of their world around them — creating technology that makes life better and accessible to everyone. We're a customer-centric company aiming to provide a reliable, enjoyable, and premium experience.
